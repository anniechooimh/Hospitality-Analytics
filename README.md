## Project Overview
This analysis explores hotel booking patterns using integrated data to uncover a comprehensive view of guest behavior and operational performance.

## Problem Statement
How can hotels leverage on booking, customer, market segment, and reservation status data to reduce cancellations, optimize occupancy, and improve revenue management?

## Research Objectives
1. Identify the Main Factors Influencing Booking Cancellations
> Analyze booking characteristics such as lead time, deposit type, customer type and market segment to determine what drives cancellations.
2. Identify the drivers of cancellations (deposit type, lead time, customer type, market segment) to estimate cancellation probability.
3. Cluster customers by type (Transient, Contract, Group, Transient-Party), deposit policy, and meal plan to identify high-value segments and tailor loyalty programs.
4. Identify occupancy trends across Resort Hotels vs. City Hotels by analyzing arrival dates, stay duration, and booking patterns.
5. Evaluate distribution channels (Direct, Corporate, TA/TO, GDS) in terms of booking volume, cancellations, and revenue contribution to guide marketing spend.
6. Study reservation statuses (Check-Out, Canceled, No-Show) to identify risk factors for no-shows and cancellations, and recommend preventive measures.
